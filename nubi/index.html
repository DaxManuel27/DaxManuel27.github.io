Nubi

Nubi is a Chrome extension for University of New Brunswick students. It puts every Brightspace deadline from your current courses into one list in Chrome's side panel, next to your class timetable and your own study events, with reminders you control.

Your deadlines and calendars stay on your computer. Nubi has no ads, no analytics and never asks for your password.

Get Nubi

Install Nubi from the Chrome Web Store, then click its toolbar icon to open the side panel. No UNB account? Choose Try Nubi with sample data on the first screen.

Try the calendar

Download the sample timetable (.ics) and import it from Nubi's Calendar tab with Import ICS file.
